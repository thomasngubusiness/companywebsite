Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

[Company] Security ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. This document is a template and should be reviewed by legal counsel before publication.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, company, email, phone number, and the details you include in enquiry, contact or application forms. We also collect limited technical data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, deliver and improve our services, communicate with you, meet legal obligations, and protect the security of our platform and clients.

3. Legal Bases

Where applicable (e.g., under GDPR), we process personal data on the bases of consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, and legal compliance.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers (such as email delivery) under appropriate safeguards, or where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted.

6. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure development practices — to protect your data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us below.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality and may use analytics cookies to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@example.com.
