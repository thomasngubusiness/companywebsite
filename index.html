    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Answers to the questions enterprises ask us most before an engagement.

    What cybersecurity services do you offer?

    We provide penetration testing (VAPT), source code review, cloud security assessments, server configuration hardening, compliance assessments and security consulting.

    How quickly can a security engagement start?

    Most engagements are scoped and kicked off within five business days, with senior-led testing throughout.

    Do you re-test after we remediate the findings?

    Yes. A validation re-test of your fixes is included at no extra cost so you can confirm issues are resolved.

    Which testing methodologies do you follow?

    Our work aligns to the OWASP Testing Guide, PTES, MITRE ATT&CK and CIS Benchmarks, combining manual depth with tooling.

    Will the report be useful to my engineers?

    Every engagement ends with an executive risk summary plus reproducible, CVSS-scored technical findings and prioritized, business-aware remediation.
