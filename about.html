Our story

From hands-on hacking to enterprise assurance

[Company] Security began with a simple conviction: most breaches are preventable, and the gap is rarely tooling — it's depth, context, and follow-through. We started as a small group of penetration testers obsessed with finding the issues automated scanners miss.

Today we deliver offensive testing, managed defense and cloud security to organizations across regulated, high-stakes industries — pairing adversarial depth with the rigor and reporting an enterprise expects.
